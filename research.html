Learning Probability of Default and Stress Testing

Luca Nocciola and Samuele Scaglioni · 2026

We analyse the probability of default of European non-financial corporations using Random Forests and assess the implications for banking-sector stress testing. Using Orbis financial statements and AnaCredit loan exposures, the paper compares Random Forests with logistic regression, maps adverse macroeconomic and sectoral scenarios into heterogeneous firm-level shocks, and transmits stressed default risk to banks through a bipartite network. The results highlight stronger scenario sensitivity, non-linear risk amplification, and the identification of tail banks.

Institutional Cleansing and Political Scars: Mafia-Related Municipal Dissolution, Electoral Participation, Political Supply, and Credit Disruption in Italy

Nordine Abidi, Ixart Miquel-Flores, and Samuele Scaglioni · June 2026

This paper studies the political and economic consequences of mafia-related municipal dissolution in Italy. Combining referendum and election results, municipality-level administrator data, and SAFE-ORBIS evidence, it documents a persistent decline in electoral participation, a contraction and reshuffling of the local political supply, and short-run deterioration in firms' credit conditions.

IMPACT: Pathway-Aware and Equity-Audited Allocation under Scarce Resources

With Hadis Anahideh · Work in progress, 2026

IMPACT is a pathway-aware decision-support framework for allocating scarce interventions under uncertainty. It separates risk identification from treatment responsiveness, estimates heterogeneous treatment effects on intermediate outcomes, and evaluates whether predicted mediator pathways are likely to change final outcomes. The education application studies financial aid using first-year GPA and earned credits as mediators and adds individual-level consistency and group-level equity diagnostics.
